# AT-004 native v5 pan and zoom Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Prove a native v5 trend line keeps its exact UTC/price anchors across pan and zoom with at least 1,000 loaded bars, and expose validated visible-range control for future linked charts.

**Architecture:** Add a small logical-range read/set API around the chart's public v5 time-scale API. Reject malformed or zero-width ranges before they reach the chart. A Chromium test loads 1,200 bars, moves through wide and narrow ranges, and compares the trend midpoint with separately rendered endpoint time/price markers while checking the original anchor record remains byte-for-byte equivalent.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Lightweight Charts 5.2.1 public `ITimeScaleApi` methods, Chromium fixture smoke.
