# Native v5 Bollinger Bands parity plan

**Goal:** Add the existing Beta Bollinger calculation as a usable, account-scoped native v5 overlay.

**Semantics:** Match `ChartStudioBeta.calculations.bollinger`: close-price SMA, population standard deviation, warm-up for the full lookback, and no future points. Defaults are period 20 and deviation 2; validate period 2–300 and deviation 0.1–10 in 0.1 steps. Three aligned upper/middle/lower series count as one indicator instance.

**Acceptance:** A Chromium contract compares all three series and the data-window middle value with Beta; covers same-bar replacement, append, history replacement, option updates, visibility, invalid options, collisions, shared capacity, cleanup, and no broker writes. Preserve existing v4 production behavior and keep native v5 isolated behind its existing opt-in gate. No Strategy Tester, backtest, Blueberry access, or broker mutation.
